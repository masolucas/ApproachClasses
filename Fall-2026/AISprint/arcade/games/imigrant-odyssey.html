WELCOME TO AMERICA!

You just landed in America with $0 and a suitcase full of snacks.
Can you turn it into $1,000,000?

Greta, Igor and Val take turns answering 20 questions about the United States, one player per question. Every right answer adds money to your savings. A wrong answer sends you back to your last safe checkpoint.

⚠️ Warning: contains jokes, banana peels, real car prices and one extremely dramatic goose.

💵 Earn as you learnEach correct answer raises your savings, from $100 up to the final $1,000,000.
🏁 Safe checkpointsQuestions 5, 10 and 15 lock in $1,000, $10,000 and $100,000.
🎯 50:50 and walk awayOne 50:50 lifeline, and you can always leave with what you have.
👕🚗🏠 Watch your life changeYour character gets better clothes, a better car and a better home with every right answer.
