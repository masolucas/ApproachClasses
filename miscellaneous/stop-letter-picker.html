STOP!

How to play STOP!

  • Draw a letter. Tap the button — a letter appears on the sign.
  • Write fast. Everyone writes one word for each category on the worksheet that starts with that letter. You have 60–90 seconds.
  • Call "Stop!" The first student to finish every category calls "Stop!" and everyone puts their pens down.
  • Score it. 20 points for a valid, unique word. 10 points if someone else wrote the same word. 0 points for a blank or invalid answer.
  • Next round. Draw a new letter and repeat. Letters already used are marked below the sign so no round repeats until every letter has had a turn.

Tip: turn off "Skip tricky letters" for higher-level classes who are ready for Q, X, and Z.
